There was one last thing I wanted to leave here.

Nargis,

Before You Go… ❤️ And now, I think this is where my little journey comes to an end. I made this website not because I wanted anything from you, but because I wanted to give you something that came from my heart. I hope you liked it. It's my first time making this and also the video. I have never worked upon such things. Every word, every picture, every little detail here carries a piece of the memories I’ve shared with you and the feelings I could never properly put into words. I like you. I know you do not. But you don’t have to feel the same, and I won’t love you any less for it. I just think you’re too beautiful of a soul to leave these words unsaid. I am sorry that I couldn't fulfil what you asked me once for. I tried but I couldn't find him. You once asked me if i could arrange a conversation with him and I failed. Maybe God brought you into my life for a reason, maybe only for a season. I don’t know what tomorrow holds. So, before it arrives, I just wanted you to know you became someone my heart is grateful to have met. You know we thank some people for merely living at the same time as we do. I thank you for the fact that I met you, that I will remember you for all my life. Today, on your birthday, I only wish one thing for you—that life is always kind to you. May you find happiness in places you never expected, may your dreams come true, and may you always have someone beside you who makes you feel loved, valued, and safe. Maybe this is goodbye. Maybe our paths will slowly become different. And perhaps, someday, I’ll become nothing more than a beautiful memory from a chapter of your life. But I hope you remember this: There was once someone who genuinely wished for nothing but your happiness. Thank you for being a part of my life. Thank you for the memories, the smiles, and even the moments that hurt. I’ll carry them quietly, and I’ll let you go with a smile. Happy Birthday. ❤️ May you always be happy. And wherever life takes you… I hope it takes you somewhere beautiful. Don't worry.! I can't leave you. Just think of me and You will always find me by your side. Goodbye. 🌙

— always
